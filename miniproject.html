About KIET

KIET is a Deemed to be University known for academic excellence, industry-aligned education and strong national rankings. It holds NAAC A+ accreditation, has NBA-accredited programs, a QS I-GAUGE Diamond rating and DSIR-SIRO recognition. The campus has advanced Centres of Excellence, a government-recognized Technology Business Incubator and modern research facilities. KIET is ranked in NIRF and IIRF and promotes innovation, entrepreneurship and holistic student development.

Our Vision

To grow into a globally recognized multidisciplinary university that is committed to skill-based education, research, innovation and ethical values, and that nurtures well-rounded individuals who serve society for sustainable development.

Our Mission

1. To give flexible, learner-centric education that builds creativity and critical thinking.
2. To promote interdisciplinary research, innovation and entrepreneurship.
3. To develop industry-ready graduates through experiential learning and international exposure.
4. To nurture globally competent and socially responsible graduates.
5. To ensure inclusive education built on integrity, ethics and environmental care.
6. To use digital innovation and emerging technologies in teaching, research and administration.
